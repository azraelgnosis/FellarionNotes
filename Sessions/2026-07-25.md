# Synopsis
- [[Pryderi ap Rhiannon|Mabrian]] catches [[Taliesen]] up on the most recent goings on.
- Caravan meeting!
- [[Petrara Umbriel|Petrara]] explains that her cousin [[Achelois Umbraiel]] warned her regarding the strangeness surrounding [[Peridotos]].
- It is decided that the caravan has to go to the city before [[Artemis Sáppheiros|Princess Artemis]] arrives with her caravan.
- But that only some should enter the city and we shouldn't stay overnight and some should go to the palace while others go to check out the city.
- Going (Palace): [[Apollo|Prince Apollo]], [[Hyacinthus]], [[Taliesen]], [[Claudia|Princess Claudia]], [[Petrara Umbriel|Petrara]], [[Elrick|Prince Elrick]], [[Alrick|Prince Alrick]], [[King's Shadow]], [[Rhedynfr]], [[Cefn ap Balin|Prince Cefn]], [[Balin ferch Balin|Princess Balin]]
- Going (City): [[Pryderi ap Rhiannon|Mabrian]], 
- Not Going: [[Hermes]], anyone else not otherwise mentioned (there are a lot of people on this caravan)
- [[Cal]] is called over by [[Alrick|Prince Alrick]] and is grilled about what he's doing with the caravan.
- [[Cal]] meets with the others and generates a cover story that he has missives for various West/South Orc clans.
- While investigating the [[Gosreg|Horrors of the Depths]], [[Issa]] notices [[Neaera]] and [[Chloris]] whispering amongst themselves. 
- Apparently they're saying that it's good that the "benevolent shapeshifter" showed up and that [[Pryderi ap Rhiannon|Mabrian]] can't be trusted.
- The kids, those rascals, arranged with the [[Gosreg|Horrors of the Depths]] to take the guise of [[Orion]] and reveal that [[Pryderi ap Rhiannon|Mabrian]] lied about [[Orion]] being the "chosen one" and instead of helping him on his quest, turned "him" in to the town guard.
- To gain their trust, [[Issa]] says he'll stab [[Pryderi ap Rhiannon|Mabrian]]. They run over to [[Pryderi ap Rhiannon|Mabrian]] and hold his arms so he can't get away.
- [[Issa]] accidentally misses and stabs [[Neaera]] instead, but heals him from dying immediately.
- The kids yell that [[Issa]] is also a traitor and run off.
- [[Elrick|Prince Elrick]] calls [[Cal]] over and dictates a love poem for [[Balin ferch Balin|Princess Balin]] for [[Cal]] to deliver. The words "glistening tusks" feature prominently.
- [[Balin ferch Balin|Princess Balin]] seems flattered and sends a return note.
## The next day
- The [[Godthief Wagon]] is at the front of the caravan.
- [[Petrara Umbriel|Petrara]] sees what appears to be a pool of water that she recognizes as a [[Coldmire Pond|kind of creature]] that disguised as a pool. Oh no!
- While attempting to sidestep the creature, [[Issa]] missteps and initiates combat!
- [[Pryderi ap Rhiannon|Mabrian]] is knocked out and submerged and begins drowning.
- The other members of the caravan begin to catch up and engage.
- After getting free and standing, [[Pryderi ap Rhiannon|Mabrian]] draws [[Caledfwlch]] for the first time in the campaign and slashes the water.... missing and doing nothing!
- Eventually, the caravan defeats the water creature.
## Outside the city
- The caravan manages to approach the outskirts of the city and set up camp.
- [[Cal]] notices a number of webs in the area. It looks like [[Drider]]s or [[Giant Spider]]s are about.
- [[Pryderi ap Rhiannon|Mabrian]] waits until before it's time to bed down and grabs [[Taliesen]] to go investigate what's up with the spider webs.
- [[Rhedynfr]] catches them immediately and points out that there are scouts for that and that they're supposed to be preparing to go into the city the next day but accepts [[Pryderi ap Rhiannon|Mabrian]]'s insistence that they'll go to sleep.
# Meanwhile
- [[Issa]] notices [[AnnieLashawn]] walking by with a cut on her arm.
- [[Issa]] investigates back where [[AnnieLashawn]] came from and finds [[Neaera]] and [[Chloris]]. They mention that [[Orion]] stabbed [[AnnieLashawn]] and ran off.
