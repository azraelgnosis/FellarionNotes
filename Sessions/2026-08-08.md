# Synopsis
- [[Pryderi ap Rhiannon|Mabrian]] awakens to [[Orion]] talking with [[Caledfwlch]].
- Since [[AnnieLashawn]] had attempted to snatch [[Orion]] - just like someone had warned - he has decided maybe we can be trusted.
- [[Taliesen]] stops by and explains that everyone going into the city is to travel via [[Apollo|Prince Apollo]]'s wagon, with the city crew jumping out before reaching the outskirts of the city.
- A plan is devised, rather than risk leaving [[Orion]] at camp where [[AnnieLashawn]] and [[Trajedie]] will have opportunity to grab him again. [[Orion]] will accompany the city crew; what's the worst that could happen?
- The [[King's Shadow]] hands one each of a pair of [[Sending Stone|Sending Stones]] to [[Petrara Umbriel|Petrara]] and [[Gwynt]]. [[Elrick]] and [[Alrick]] explain that they are for communication, in case anyone needs help.
- To break the tension, [[Gwynt]] tells a story about mountains
- Mansion Crew: [[Petrara Umbriel|Petrara]], [[King's Shadow]], [[Elrick]], [[Alrick]], [[Cefn ap Balin|Prince Cefn]], [[Apollo|Prince Apollo]], [[Balin ferch Balin|Princess Balin]], [[Claudia|Princess Claudia]], [[Hyacinthus]], [[Taliesen]], [[Rhedynfr]]
- City Crew: [[Pryderi ap Rhiannon|Mabrian]], [[Cal]], [[Gwynt]], [[Issa]], [[Palug]], [[Coron]], (and secretly [[Orion]])
## Making our way
- The gang notices that there are a number of webs along the path. Not super dense but apparently distributed to sense disruption.
- The city crew make it into town sneakily. But as there are passersby, they stare at [[Orion]].
- As we are making our way, the crew witness a large object that was being moved, it falls and crushes a person. [[Pryderi ap Rhiannon|Mabrian]] and [[Issa]] run over to help the lady.
- There are whispers suggesting that this person is doomed for some reason.
- She explains that no one who stays overnight leaves but there is nothing that they can do, as "they" have their children.
## Meanwhile, at the mayor's manor
- The mansion crew arrive at the mayor's manor. They are greeted by a couple [[Melpomine Peridoto]] and [[Jason Peridoto]]
- In small groups, the royals go to refresh themselves. Before [[Petrara Umbriel|Petrara]]'s turn, The [[King's Shadow]] and [[Rhedynfr]] appear and explain that all of the royals have disappeared.
- [[Petrara Umbriel|Petrara]] uses the [[Sending Stone]] to contact the City Crew and demand that they come to the manor.
- The gang split up to explore the manor.
- Team Stompers: [[Gwynt]], [[Orion]], [[Cal]], [[Pryderi ap Rhiannon|Mabrian]], [[Palug]]
- Team Scrappers: [[Petrara Umbriel|Petrara]], [[King's Shadow]], [[Rhedynfr]], [[Coron]], [[Issa]]
- Team Stompers overhear a pair of people lamenting that they have to betray the new guests, but how else will they keep their children safe?
- Team Scrappers find a child's room where all of the furniture has been covered by blankets.
- Team Scrappers encounter a guard who begins to lead them to the baths. They instead decide to attack the guard who calls a second. 
- They kill the first and interrogate the second. The second guard tells them of a secret path before also being killed.
- Team Stompers discover a locked room with the missing companion's stuff.
- Team Scrappers commit several murders of guards along their way.
- Team Scrappers encounter [[Penelope|a servant]] who offers to help them.
- [[Penelope]] and [[Petrara Umbriel|Petrara]] sing a hole in the wall and wind their way through a series of rooms.
- Team Stompers encounter Team Scrappers before long.
- [[Penelope]] leads everyone to a trap door that leads to some sort of basement area with two wagons with humanoid webbed bundles.
